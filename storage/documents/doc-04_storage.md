## Escalation Triage Matrix
- Tier 1 (Critical): Unauthorized exfiltration of credentials. Response SLA: 60 minutes.
- Tier 2 (Elevated): Unauthorized access attempt blocked by access control rule. Response SLA: 4 hours.
- GDPR Article 33: Supervisory authority notification within 72 hours.
